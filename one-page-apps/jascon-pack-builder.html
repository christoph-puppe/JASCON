JASCON Advice Pack Builder

How this works

An advice pack is the file a vendor publishes so that its customers can take the vendor's recommendations into their JASCON set: which requirement a product helps with, which clauses, what to configure, and which parameter values the vendor suggests. A pack holds your product as one or more Components and nothing else, so importing it cannot change a customer's requirements, tailorings or assessments. The specification is decision D25; the handbook explains it in chapter 9.7.

  1. Say who you are: the origin all your identifiers start with, the publisher, the pack and its release.
  2. Describe the product as a Component and each recommendation as a capability. Loading the catalogue the recommendations refer to is optional; with it you pick requirements and clauses from a list, and clauses and suggested values are checked.
  3. The panel on the right checks the pack while you type, with the same rules the JASCON app runs when a customer imports it.
  4. Sign the release with your Ed25519 key, in this page or with openssl, so that customers can tell it comes from you.
  5. Download the ZIP and publish it together with your public key.

Everything stays in this page. Your draft is kept in this browser's storage, your key only in the page's memory: a reload forgets it. To change a published pack, open it, change it and give the release a new version.
