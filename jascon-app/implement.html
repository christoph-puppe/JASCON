Views

A component says what a system, service or product is. An implementation is the edge from a component to a requirement: who is responsible, how far it is done, and on which authorization an inherited edge rests.

⚙Advice

A vendor's recommendation is a capability on the vendor's Component: the requirement and clauses it is about, a text, and the parameter values the vendor suggests. It is a claim. That a requirement is met is said only by an Implementation of your own component, which adopts the recommendation by citing it. A product's advice applies to your component because the component contains the product: the product is one of its members.
