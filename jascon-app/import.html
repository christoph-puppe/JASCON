?What is carried, and how

A catalog. Each control becomes a Requirement, each group a Set, and the catalog itself the root Set. The text of the statement part becomes the clauses; an insert of a parameter becomes a {param:} token, and the parameter is declared on the clause that names it. A link to another control becomes a relation.

The generic rules make every nested control a Requirement of its own, with a relation to its parent. The modality is unspecified, because OSCAL has no field for the binding force. Props and parts the kernel has no field for are held verbatim in a facet under your namespace, with a pinned schema; nothing is dropped without a line in the report.

The preset for the BSI Anwenderkatalog Grundschutz++ follows the converter of this repository: a nested control is a clause of its parent, the modal verb becomes the modality, the BSI props go to the standard facets, and the security level becomes baseline Sets. Its ids are minted under the namespace the converter uses.

The preset for NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5 follows the converter of this repository: a withdrawn control is not converted and its successor names it; every statement item with prose is a clause with modality must; assessment objectives and methods, the administrative data of parameters and the guidance go to facets under the NIST namespace. The baselines are profiles and are imported as profiles.

The preset for a CIS Benchmark follows the converter of this repository: every rule is a Requirement with one clause, whose text is the rule's title because the export leaves the statement empty; the audit and remediation text and the marking automated or manual go to the facet assessment-criteria; the profiles become baseline Sets and the CIS Controls cross-references Mappings. The Checks tool drafts machine checks from the audit text.

A profile. The controls it includes become a Set, and a Tailoring selects that Set. Excluded controls become excludes. A set-parameter becomes an operation on every clause that declares the parameter, if the value lies in the declared space; a value outside it needs a Deviation and is listed, not written.

Not carried, and counted in the report: alters, the merge directive, pattern matching, the inclusion of child controls. An alter changes text, and somebody has to decide whether that is editorial or substantive; use the Tailor tool for it.

Imported content stays under its publisher's terms. State them in the field for the licence; they are written into the provenance of the bundle.
