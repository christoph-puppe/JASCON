?How to read an object

Every object has three layers, kept strictly apart.

Kernel: identity (an id that is a URI, compared as a string and never fetched), version, lifecycle, statements with a binding force, obligated parties, typed parameters, membership, aliases and relations.

Facets: registered extensions pinned to a schema. Each says which computations it may change; a tool that does not understand such a facet must stop, never guess.

Annotations: notes for display. They are left out of the semantic digest and may be removed without changing what the object means.

Two digests seal each object in the manifest. The package digest binds the delivered bytes; the semantic digest binds the meaning. A signature therefore survives repackaging, and a change of meaning has nowhere to hide.

The lattice on the right orders the binding force. Moving up is a tightening and free. Moving down or across is a weakening and needs a Deviation, unless the authority itself publishes the Tailoring.
