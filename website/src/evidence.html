Evidence

What was measured, on what, and what was not

Three rules govern the project. Every claim is labelled as measured, designed for or hypothesised. No mechanism enters the specification without a published catalogue that demands it. Every prohibition names the counted failure it prevents. This page applies the first rule to the project itself.

04 Limits

What is not proven

These are the open points on {{ facts.measured.date }}. Each of them is also a place where outside work counts for more than the maintainer's.

No third-party user open

A search on 10 November 2026 found no mention of JASCON outside its own repository. No authority publishes in it and no vendor reads it. The measured conversions and the defects found in published catalogues can be used without adopting the format.

One author behind both validators open

The Python reference and the PowerShell twin were written by the project author with AI assistance from the same sources. Their independence is limited to language and runtime. The gate measures whether the specification determines an implementation. It does not measure a stranger's weekend.

Path coverage is not value arrival open, backlog 41

The coverage reports count source paths with a declared destination. Whether each value arrives was measured on one corpus, the NIST example system: 394 of 835 leaf values occur verbatim in the bundle, and 122 content values do not arrive at all. The other ten corpora are unmeasured.

The first Risk corpus stays in its first state open

OSCAL's risk data has no owner, no single level and no acceptance date. Risks converted from it cannot leave the state identified, so risk coverage on real converted data shows every requirement as uncovered. Coverage with a treated risk is shown on the example bundle only.

Check closes its shape on two platforms open

The fit of the Check type was counted on Linux hosts and Kubernetes. No check against a cloud API was counted, nothing in the repository runs a collector, and the worked example ran against a stub. 29 of 510 counted rules do not fit the declarative form and stay manual. The state in full.

2.0.0 is a candidate, not a release open

Nothing is left to decide, and both validators are green on all {{ n.vectors }} vectors of the candidate. The tag is the author's step and has not been taken. Until then the released version is 1.0.0, and the shapes of 2.0.0 can still change.

Not every 1.0.0 bundle is a 2.0.0 bundle decided

Until rc.2 the 2.0.0 schema accepted everything 1.0.0 accepted. Since rc.3 the date a deviation was opened must be a date, and the due date of an action a typed point in time or duration. A 1.0.0 bundle with free text in either field has to convert it. All five shipped bundles and the example folder conform. A pattern with a Unicode category follows the Unicode database of the machine it runs on, so a code point assigned later can give two verdicts on two machines; an author who needs one verdict writes the range.

Advice packs rest on one example file designed for

No catalogue authority publishes vendor advice. For such data the promotion rule asks for a standard that requires the record, or for two open implementations whose content was counted. Advice packs have neither. The clause-level field on a capability is backed by a single fictional component definition from NIST's examples, with three recommendations, two clause-level entries and two suggested parameter values; the product-version field is backed by nothing counted. Both entered the kernel on the author's decision against that rule, and the specification records it that way. No vendor has published a pack, the pack builder imports OSCAL component definitions, but no published one has been run through it, and the census of public component definitions is still owed.

Known defects in shipped examples open, backlog 46 and 55

The example attestation is illustrative: its subject digests match no example object. The method payload of an Assessment is not yet validated against its pinned facet schema, and the three in the repository would fail.

Governance is one person open

The handbook describes a federated registry and a steward for the standard library. Neither exists. Today there is one maintainer in personal capacity, a public decision register, and the conformance corpus.

The full queue with counts: the spec-feedback backlog. Items enter with evidence and leave through an entry in the decision register.

05 Prior art

Most of JASCON is not new

A survey of the neighbouring formats, read from their public repositories on 10 November 2026. No neighbour's data was converted and no feature was tested against an implementation, so the cells say what a schema or its documentation shows.

What no neighbour was found to combine

  • Two digests per object, one over the delivered bytes and one over the canonical content, so that an attestation survives repackaging.
  • A partial order of modality against which tailoring is checked, with weakening routed through a recorded Deviation.
  • A risk record joined to control selection, so that the requirements resting on no risk are computed.
  • Extensions that fail closed: an extension declares which computations it changes, and a tool that does not understand it must stop.

How far that claim goes

Each of the four has partial precedent. The claim concerns the combination and is only as strong as the survey. Gemara and CycloneDX Attestations are the closest neighbours, and a reader from either community will ask why JASCON does not extend theirs. The attestation gap is narrow: in-toto has no predicate that attests a requirement, and a JASCON attestation expressed as one would reach an existing verifier ecosystem.
