Privacy

The pages of this site set no cookies, embed no analytics and load nothing from other hosts. Fonts, scripts and images come from this site.

  • Server logs. The host that delivers these files processes your IP address to do so and may keep access logs. The publisher of this site does not evaluate them.
  • Colour scheme and style. When you press the light and dark button or pick a style, the choice is stored in your browser's local storage. It is not transmitted.
  • The digest demo on the home page computes in your browser. The text you type stays there.
  • The app and the reader keep the bundles you load in your browser (IndexedDB and local storage). Nothing is uploaded.
  • Suggestions from a language model. Two pages of the app can send text to a model service. They do so only after you enter an API key of your own, they show what they send, and the key is held in memory only. The service you choose then processes that text under its own terms.
  • Links to GitHub lead to github.com, which applies its own privacy statement.
